The **Generate** tab makes new practice and test questions from Manitoba curricular outcomes. It is experimental: turn it on under **Settings -> More -> Question generator (experimental)**.

Generate has problem types for these courses:

- Grade 9 Mathematics (10F)
- Grade 10 Introduction to Applied and Pre-Calculus Mathematics (20S)
- Grade 11 Pre-Calculus Mathematics (30S)
- Grade 12 Pre-Calculus Mathematics (40S)

Every question comes with its answer, and you can print the answers in an answer key. Generated questions are not saved to a bank. They go onto a worksheet in Generate, and from there into a test.

## Choose a Problem Type

The left pane lists the problem types for the chosen **Course**, grouped by unit or outcome. Each type shows its outcome codes. It also shows **options** if it has extra settings, and **written** if it can only be free response. Use **Search problem types or outcomes** to filter by name or outcome code, such as `9.N.2`.

Click a problem type to open its card:

- **Number of questions**: how many questions to make.
- **Preset choices**: **Easy**, **Medium**, or **Hard**. The text under the buttons describes what each level means for this type.
- **Question type**: **Free response** or **Multiple choice**. Types with no sensible wrong answers stay free response.
- **Options**: settings for that type, such as the size of numbers, the leading coefficient, or the word-problem context. Changing a preset resets its options to that level's defaults.

The preview shows exactly the questions that will be added. Use the buttons on each preview question to get new numbers for it or to remove it. Then choose:

- **Add**: add the previewed questions to the worksheet and close the card.
- **Add & Continue**: add them and keep the card open so you can add another set, for example at a different level.

## The Worksheet

Each time you add from a card, it becomes a section on the worksheet. A section shows its instruction, problem type, number of questions, level, and options.

- **Instruction**: printed once above the section's questions, such as "Factor completely:". It is filled in automatically when every question starts the same way. Type in the box to change it, or clear it to go back to the automatic text.
- **Columns**: how many questions go in each row on the test. **Auto** chooses based on question length.
- **Edit**: reopen the card with the section's settings and questions.
- **↑ / ↓**: move the section. **✕** removes it.

The toolbar applies to the whole worksheet:

- **Written / Multiple choice**: switch every section at once.
- **↻ New numbers**: make new questions for every section, keeping all of their settings.
- **Answers**: show the answer under each question.
- **Clear worksheet**: remove every section.

The worksheet is saved in this browser and stays there when you leave the tab.

## Put the Questions on a Test

Use **Add to…** to put the worksheet's questions on the current test or a saved test. Build opens after they are added.

Each section prints as one numbered item, with its instruction once and its questions lettered a), b), c). Sections in a row with the same instruction share a number, and their letters continue. In Generate, a section like that shows a dashed border and **Continues 2**. See [Questions from Generate](./test-builder.md#questions-from-generate) for the printed layout.

To change a generated question that is already on a test, choose **Edit in Generator** in Build. Its card opens in Generate. Change the settings or numbers, then choose **Replace in test**.
