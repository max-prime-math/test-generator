Manitoba Problem Atlas

Every kind of problem a question generator could produce for this course, taken from Manitoba's achievement indicators and tagged with the specific learning outcomes each one assesses.

Outcomes and their wording: Grades 9 to 12 Mathematics: Manitoba Curriculum Framework of Outcomes (2014). Problem types are derived from the achievement indicators. Text types can be generated as written or multiple-choice questions. Graph types need a graph in the question or the answer. Proof types are better kept as hand-written bank questions.
