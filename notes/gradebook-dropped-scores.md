# Dropped score policies

Teacher task: configure separate lowest-score exclusions for tests, quizzes, and other categories, preview the effect on students, then apply or reverse the policy while preserving recorded marks.

The section stores a count for each category. Each student's eligible assessment percentages are ranked independently. Up to the configured count is dropped, keeping at least one counted assessment per category. Missing participates only under the section's zero policy; Ungraded, Excused, Absent, Incomplete, invalid denominators and unfinished alternative marks do not consume drops. Individual alternative denominators and bonus marks are respected. Ties use administered date, creation date, then stable ID.

One calculation returns counted category totals, the weighted/fallback course grade, and excluded assessment IDs. Overview, Student category totals, grade filters, report totals, and Overview CSV share it. Raw assessment summaries and question entry retain their recorded marks. Explicit Dropped labels explain exclusions; Missing remains a follow-up state even when dropped. Raw Scores CSV retains its existing fields and appends droppedByPolicy.

Overview's Category weights & drops disclosure hosts an explicit Save workflow with current policy, pending rules, affected-student count, per-student grade comparison and exclusion preview. Drafts recover in this browser across pane changes and reload. Stale policy edits preserve the draft and require loading the current policy. Saving merges the latest section fields, changes only its policy and recovery version, preserves score Undo, and rolls back apparent changes on a browser write failure. The previous saved configuration persists with the section and can be restored, including after reload. Turning off drops requires saving and keeps all recorded marks.

The additive section fields normalize old data to no drops. JSON backups, existing record sync, section conflict review, archive/restore, and previous-policy recovery include them. Bank questions, saved tests, frozen assessment questions, score states and question scores are unchanged. Already-open reports remain snapshots.

Validation includes numerical policy tests with unequal denominators, weights and points fallback, drop-count limits, blank/zero/special states, alternatives, bonuses, stable ties, grade-filter/report/export agreement, backup/record round trips and merges. Browser tasks cover preview/save/cancel/reload, storage failure, stale policy conflict, disabling/restoring policies, preserved score Undo and editing dropped marks, Student details, reports, CSV, mobile sizing, and calculation diagnostics at 120 students × 40 assessments. Existing gradebook regression checks are reused. No teacher usability observation has been performed.

Limits: policies drop lowest percentages, with a fixed keep-one floor. They do not select the combination that maximizes final grade, add per-assessment protection, or override Missing policy. The last two saved configurations are retained. Unsaved policy drafts are browser-local; saved policies and recovery configurations travel with Gradebook backups and folder sync.
